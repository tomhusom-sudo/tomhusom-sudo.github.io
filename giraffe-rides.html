Model HT-GR-01

The Giraffe Ride Saddle

Some animals were not built for ordinary saddles. Honest Tom's Giraffe Rides has solved that very specific problem with a properly considered, field-tested-looking solution.

The HT-GR-01 combines a padded non-slip seat, a safety handle, and an easy-mount climbing step into one handsome piece of adventure equipment. It is intended for the rider who has looked up at a giraffe and thought, there must be a better way.

  • Comfort first Designed for giraffe and rider.
  • Long-neck balance Extra securing strap for the unusual geometry involved.
  • Easy mounting Integrated climbing step, because that part matters.
  • All-weather build Materials selected for outdoor adventure conditions.
