The Idea

You already know the routine: haul a tree out for December, drag it to the curb in January, and stare at an empty porch the other ten months of the year.

Honest Tom's Interchangeable Tree System fixes that with one simple idea — a single weighted pot that stays put, and full-size artificial trees that drop in and swap out in seconds. Spruce for the holidays. Bare branches for October. Blazing maple for fall. Same pot, every season.

The whole system ships in one box. The tree collapses flat for shipping and stores entirely inside the empty pot when it's not in use. No attic. No garage rafters. No tangle.

Authentic
Design
Born on a Real Porch

This isn't a focus-group product. It started when Tom set a full artificial spruce into a heavy planter on his own deck — partly because it looked great, and partly because it gave him a little year-round privacy from the neighbors' side window.

It worked. It stood through prairie wind, looked good in every season, and never needed watering. The only thing missing was a system: a pot built to take ballast, a trunk sleeve made for quick swaps, and trees designed to collapse into the pot itself. So he built one.

That's the second job this tree does: it's an instant evergreen privacy screen. No fence permit, no waiting five years for an arborvitae to grow in. Set the pot, drop the tree, and the sightline is gone — on a deck, a balcony, or right in front of that one window.

How It Works
1

Set the Pot

Place the heavy-duty pot wherever you want a tree — porch, patio, entryway, or living room. Indoors or out, rain or shine.

2

Add Ballast

Pour sand or gravel into the open ballast slot — or drop in Tom's cast iron Bal-Trunk weights — and it's not going anywhere, even in a prairie wind.

3

Swap the Season

Slide the tree trunk into the sleeve and you're done. When the season turns, pull it, collapse it, store it in the pot, and drop in the next one.

Three Sizes

One system, three statements. The full-size porch tree anchors an entry or blocks a sightline. The mid-size flanks a doorway or warms a corner inside. The accent size dresses a step, a rail, or a tabletop-height nook.

Every size uses the same drop-in trunk sleeve, takes the same ballast, and stores its own tree inside the pot. Mix sizes for a staggered porch line — the matched planters do the design work for you.

The Palm Edition

Who says it has to be a pine? The Palm Edition drops a full artificial palm into the same weighted pot — instant Caribbean, zero humidity required. Year-round tropical for sunrooms, pool decks, and patios, or a winter mood-lift for the snowbird who stayed home.

It swaps with every other tree in the system, and it comes with its own twist on the ballast: Caribbean Bal-Trunks — the same stackable cast iron weights, painted in island brights instead of vintage steamer brown.

Same pot. Same sleeve. Different latitude.
