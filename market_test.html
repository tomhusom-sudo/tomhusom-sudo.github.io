Why honest?

Aberdeen has one grocery store. It's Walmart. Kessler's and Ken's are fine for what they are, but fresh produce isn't their thing.

Tom is a neighbor who got tired of it. The idea is simple: a weekly menu of what's actually worth buying this week, sourced after you order so nothing goes to waste, sold at a fair price.

No subscription boxes. No 40-item catalog. No produce that was picked two weeks ago. Just what Tom knows is excellent right now.

What this is not
  • ✗ A subscription box you can't cancel
  • ✗ 300 items and a confusing website
  • ✗ Produce that sat in a warehouse
  • ✗ A stranger who doesn't know Aberdeen
  • ✓ Tom. With good stuff. Every Friday.
The person behind it

Tom Husom

Aberdeen local. Spent years watching the same tired produce rotate through the same tired shelves. Honest Tom's Market is the direct result — a weekly fresh food service built around the idea that Aberdeen deserves better.

Tom sources from regional distributors and local farms, posts the menu every Tuesday, and has your order ready Friday morning. If you have a question, text him.
